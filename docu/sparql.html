Query

Eight queries over a sample of the geo-lod graph. They run in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere. Edit a query and press Run.

There is no SPARQL endpoint and no server. The graph is fetched as a static file and parsed in your browser with rdflib under Pyodide, so an archived copy of the repository stays queryable. The sample holds 19,730 triples (The ontology files, the full CI and SISAL site layers, every 6th SISAL dating point and every 50th EPICA observation with its time position and MIS membership. visuals/sample_graph.py writes it.) Download the graph.

1. Which classes are used, and how often?

The instances of every class in the sample, most frequent first. The three strands show up as IceCoreObservation (EPICA), Cave and AgeControlPoint (SISAL) and CIFindspot (campanian ignimbrite).

.rq

2. Which own classes reach the CIDOC CRM family?

A class is anchored when a chain of rdfs:subClassOf axioms leads from it into CIDOC CRM, CRMsci, CRMgeo or CRMarchaeo. The property path follows the chain through the bridging axioms (SOSA, PROV and SKOS classes on the way).

.rq

3. Methane observations in a marine isotope stage

EPICA Dome C observations, each linked to a time position and a MIS stage. The query lists methane values with the stage they fall in and the depth they were measured at.

.rq

4. Where does an observation come from?

The route from one observation to the real objects it speaks about: the ice core it samples and the drilling site where it took place, with the data source it was derived from.

.rq

5. Caves with archaeological screening

SISAL caves with elevation and the number of oxygen isotope samples, restricted to those screened for archaeology.

.rq

6. Age control points per dating method

Radiometric and other dating points in the SISAL sample, grouped by method.

.rq

7. Campanian Ignimbrite findspots by spatial type

Findspots of the CI tephra, their certainty level and the literature they come from.

.rq

8. Resources typed as CRMarchaeo stratigraphic volume units

Archaeologically relevant caves and findspots carry a CRMarchaeo type directly in the data, next to their own class. The query lists them with their geometry resource.

.rq
