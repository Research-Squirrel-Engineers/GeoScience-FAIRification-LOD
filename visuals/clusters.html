Model clusters

The data model drawn in slices instead of as one graph. Each page shows the classes of one cluster, the relations that leave it as dashed edge nodes, and figures from the data. The whole model is on the data model page.

  • Archives and sampling (planned): Where the material comes from; ice cores, drilling sites, caves, speleothems and the samples cut from them. 12 classes
  • Observation and measurement (planned): What was measured, in which quantity, and how a value was smoothed afterwards. 13 classes
  • Chronology and age: How a depth becomes an age; dating points, age models, the ages they give one sample, and the breaks in the growth. 10 classes
  • Marine isotope stages (planned): The stage scheme observations are placed in, and the competing boundary sources behind it. 7 classes
  • Archaeology and the Campanian Ignimbrite (planned): Cave sites and CI findspots with archaeological evidence, and the context vocabulary that classifies them. 4 classes
  • Sources and provenance (planned): The citable sources and the catalogue that gathers the datasets. 2 classes

Every geolod: class belongs to exactly one cluster (46 classes); the assignment is visuals/clusters.yaml, and the build stops when a class is missing or listed twice.
